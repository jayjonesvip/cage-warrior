THE FIRST BELL · 2009

ORIGINS OF
CAGE GRIND

Cage Grind began years before its current name. In 2009, Jay Jones launched CageWars.net: a persistent browser game about creating an MMA fighter, building a career, and climbing toward a championship.

THE ORIGINAL GAME

CAGEWARS.NET → ONLINECAGEFIGHTING.COM

CageWars.net was renamed OnlineCageFighting.com in 2010. Its dark metal presentation, public rankings, fighter economy, persistent careers, and living activity feed became the earliest version of the world Cage Grind continues today.

The surviving pages and screenshots were recovered from the Internet Archive. They preserve product history—not the original PHP source or a specification for rebuilding it.

COMMERCIAL MILESTONE · AUGUST 2009 ONE PAID PRODUCT PLACEMENT · $150

HISTORICAL MECHANICS

Confirmed in archived CageWars and Online Cage Fighting pages.

  • Asynchronous player challenges and opponent scouting
  • Strength, Size, Skill, Endurance, health, experience, and popularity
  • Sparring, cage fights, nine weight classes, rankings, and champions
  • Earnings, level-gated purchases, lifestyle items, and bounties
  • Teams, smack talk, fighter statuses, and a live world-news feed

CURRENT CAGE GRIND SYSTEMS

The modern game carries the intent forward with its own rules and architecture.

  • Power, Speed, Chin, Cardio, Energy, Health, and Focus
  • Tactical fight plans, round decisions, training, recovery, and injuries
  • Hustles, relationships, messages, gear, sponsors, and career pressure
  • A mobile-first interface with persistent local careers
  • A shared Cage Feed, fighter identities, opponents, and world championship

THE NORTH STAR

BUILD A FIGHTER. LIVE THE CAREER.

Preserve the feeling of starting unknown, making consequential choices, becoming part of a living fight world, and earning a place at the top. The original implementation belongs to its era; the player fantasy is timeless.
