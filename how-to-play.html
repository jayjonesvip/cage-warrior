How to Play

Build your fighter. Make your calls. Earn your place in the cage.

1. Create your fighter

Choose your name, hometown, avatar, and fighting style. Build your starting attributes: Power, Speed, Chin, and Cardio. Your fighter’s identity is locked in when you start your career.

2. Pick a fight and a plan

Visit Fight to choose a Cage Circuit opponent or a fighter in the roster, grouped by level from highest to lowest then by win percentage, total wins, and head-to-head results. Check the Tale of the Tape before committing. Set your pace, offense, and tactics: stay with your style or adapt to the matchup.

Fights use Energy and Health. Both recover over time, so check your HUD before stepping back in.

3. Make your daily fights count

You receive 12 fights each day, resetting at your local midnight. Win five qualifying fights in a row during the day to earn three extra fights, once per day. Keep the streak going to ten for a public CEO shoutout and +5 Aura, capped at 100, also once per day.

Win against an opponent at your level or higher, to qualify. Repeat-opponent limits apply. A loss or ineligible win resets the streak, but rewards already earned stay yours. Beating a lower-level opponent does not count toward Daily Heat.

4. Build your career

Earn XP to level up and spend Attribute Points on permanent improvements. Wins over higher-level fighters award two Attribute Points. Same-level wins, including Cage Circuit wins, award one. Repeat-opponent reward limits apply.

Easy wins against lower-level fighters give no XP or Attribute Points and can cost Followers and Aura. Check the listed rewards before choosing your opponent.

Home tracks your XP, Aura, sponsor, Victory Pack, and Daily Heat progress. Followers attract sponsors; Aura also changes your cosmetic Fight Skin.

5. Collect and equip gear

Claim the Daily Drop and earn Victory Packs through qualifying wins. Drops reveal undiscovered items as they unlock, without duplicates. New gear automatically equips when its category has an open slot.

  • Fight Gear boosts attributes.
  • Bling boosts your effective Aura.
  • Property & Rides improves Energy recovery.
  • Lifestyle improves Health recovery.

Equip up to two items per category in Gear.

6. Scout in Open Gym

Select a roster fighter to create a sparring clone. Set your fight plan and run a one-round spar for a grade and coaching advice. Sparring costs no Energy and does not change your record, rewards, or streaks.

Choose another scout or use Rematch to try a new approach.

7. Stay in the conversation

Cage Feed brings you fighter posts, callouts, and career reactions. Share your invite from Home: when a new invited fighter creates their career and completes their first fight, you earn a guaranteed drop.

Keep your browser save and recovery information safe. Clearing site data can remove your local career and session.
